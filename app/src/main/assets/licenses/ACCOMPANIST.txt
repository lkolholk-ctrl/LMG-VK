manual scroll/seek handling, clock callback freshness, viewport/blur guards,
font-scale measurement updates, TTML offset-time units and zero-duration syllable handling,
density-aware karaoke fill feather, staggered character lift and emphasis,
line-end lift release, expanded character spacing, merged vocal-gap detection
and audio-position-based waiting dots. Further adaptations: spatial smoothing of lift,
direct per-character emphasis without rotation and word-local expansion compensation,
cached glyph geometry and animation buffers, inactive redraw suppression,
fixed instrumental spacing and vocal follow-anchor correction.
Further changes: subpixel line placement, drag-only spring suspension and
fill-position-based glyph lift independent of the emphasis schedule.
Further changes: cached viewport fade, optional blur animation suppression,
automatic layer composition and independently timed line activity transitions.
Further changes: cached supersampled glyph and shadow masks for fractional lift
without per-frame text rasterization or glyph rotation.
